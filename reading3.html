Reading 3

The graphic novels of motherhood in crisis have shown a side of life I never really knew about. Starting with Memenatu’s story, it was tragic about the loss of her child, but when she couldn’t urinate the amount of concern increased and felt like a terrible outcome to experience after the loss of her child. Once she received help from the hospital and gained a sense of hope again it felt like there was a chance for her to live her life again. The feeling that the people gave her is what all hospitals should do with mothers who are going through a hard time after the loss of a child and after the urinary track makes it difficult for them to use the basic functions their body does. Heela’s story is one that is tragic but does help with the perspective of the health care system and how it could help. The idea of having a child after rape is a terrible thing and the idea that people thought abortion can kill the mother really showed how there was no information given to the people. Once she got to the hospital it looked like everything was going to work out for her, even if she couldn’t continue schooling the fact that they helped her give her hope and allow people to see the use of medical health care. Kadiatu’s story is a story of dread and sorrow. After losing 7 children it looked hopeless for her and the people made her a social pereia which made her feel worse about herself but once she got the hospital there was a sliver of hope for the 8th child. After a week of surgeries, she covered and had her child but once he became sick, she knew of the resources she had, and it felt like a breath of fresh air for her. Tetteh’s story shows how women are treated in the justice system and the circumstances she went through feel unfair and unjust. The fact that the man that she was with beats her because of his own bad decision was just the start of the unfair treatment that the world gives her. The moment she tries to protect herself she kills him and is put to jail and with a child comes soon. The fact that she was pushed down a hill while she was pregnant, she worried that the baby with be born deformed but after being transferred to a hospital she was relieved that he was healthy. She still served her time and lost her child to the system. This life shows how unfair the justice system treats women in their society. These stories were eye opening and gave me a lot to think about when it comes to motherhood in environments that were made to put them down. Hopefully with the use of graphic novels like these more people will understand the hardships and learn to do better for women and for the future mothers of this world.
